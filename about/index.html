A hometown family
with a heart for Bethel

Brown’s Slough · Bethel, Alaska

543 The Clean Sweep is an Alaska Native-owned cleaning business created and co-owned by Chelsea Hoffman and Teddy Street. Chelsea grew up in Bethel and has lived here her whole life, and together they are raising their three daughters here.

Our family takes pride in Bethel, and that pride carries into the work 543 does. Whether we’re cleaning a family’s home, a local office, or a community space, we want to leave it looking like someone cared. That’s our way of adding a little more sparkle to the town our girls call home.
